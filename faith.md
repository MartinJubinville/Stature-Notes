# New Life of Faith

## Homebase of Operations

### Grace and Justification

**CCC 142** *By his Revelation*, "the invisible God, from the fullness of his love, addresses men as his friends, and moves among them, in order to invite and receive them into his own company." The adequate response to this invitation is faith.

**CCC 143** *By faith*, man completely submits his intellect and his will to God. With his whole being man gives his assent to God the revelaer. Sacred Scripture calls this human response to God, the author of revelation, "the obedience of faith".
